# 11. Primary Screens and Imports

> Part of the [Maicora MVP Specification](./index.md).

---

## Primary Screens

Minimum:

1. Login
2. Tenant selector
3. Agent workspace / Home
4. Customers list
5. Customer detail
6. Customer form
7. Products list
8. Product detail
9. Product form
10. Pro Pricing / Costing
11. Inventory overview
12. Inventory product detail / movement history
13. Inventory adjustment
14. Invoices list
15. Invoice detail
16. Invoice draft/review
17. Fiscal settings
18. CSD/PAC setup
19. Proposal review
20. ChangeSet/history
21. Tenant/user/permission settings
22. AI settings

---

## Imports

MVP:

- Customer CSV import
- Product CSV import
- Opening stock CSV import

Agent-assisted field mapping is desirable but not a first technical milestone.
